Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This application ("the App") is a personal-use tool that generates and publishes short-form video content to a TikTok account that the developer controls.

Data We Collect

The App accesses only the TikTok account authorization tokens (OAuth access token and refresh token) required to publish video content on behalf of the account owner. The App does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any third party.

How Data Is Used

Authorization tokens are used solely to upload video content to the connected TikTok account via the official TikTok Content Posting API. Tokens are stored locally and are not transmitted to any third party.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

Data Retention

Authorization tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the App and can be deleted at any time by revoking access in the TikTok account settings.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer directly.
